# Two-section design system

The page follows the executive-summary and company-positioning content of the supplied PRD. Evergreen ink, warm neutral panels, restrained lime accents, generous editorial typography, and an original conceptual energy landscape create the corporate visual identity.

`Header`, `Logo`, and `Footer` are shared components. The main page has exactly two semantic sections. Executive Summary contains the hero, introduction, three strategic impressions, and stakeholder audiences. Company Positioning contains identity, mission/vision, principle cards, brand personality, and the statement on verified claims.

Mobile navigation uses an expanded-state button and links to those same two sections. Layout grids collapse to one column, focus states are visible, anchor scroll respects the sticky header, and reduced-motion preferences disable smooth scrolling and animation. No analytics, cookie banner, forms, or additional navigation destinations are included.

`lib/profile.ts` holds typed PRD content and an optional validated Sanity singleton read. Conceptual imagery depicts no Ojasvi-owned facility.
